# Boplo Landing — Product Direction

## Decision

Boplo is pivoting from the dating-reply assistant to **Boplo Landing**: a mobile-first relocation companion for international students and early-career movers (18–28).

## Core promise

Tell Boplo where, when, and why you are moving. It tells you whether the move is realistic for your budget and gives you an actionable plan for what to do next.

## Initial product

Launch with London, Barcelona, and Amsterdam. A user creates a move by providing their origin, destination, move date, reason, monthly budget, savings, and priorities.

The free result gives a move-reality signal, a high-level budget, the three largest risks, and a first-week checklist preview. A one-off paid plan unlocks the full first-month budget, neighbourhood direction, pre-arrival timeline, first-72-hours plan, multi-city comparison, and a shareable plan.

## Future defensibility

After the decision product works, add a small, manually vetted local-advisor network per city. Advisors can provide paid calls around university life, neighbourhoods, housing shortlists, and first-week setup. They must not provide legal, immigration, tax, or financial advice unless professionally qualified.

Then add arrival reminders, move cohorts, community, and city partnerships.

## Business model

- Free: one high-level city plan.
- Boplo Plus: one-off full Move Plan, target price £8.99.
- Advisor calls: £15–£35, with a marketplace fee.

Avoid subscriptions initially. The customer has a time-bound, high-stakes move and a clear reason to buy once.

## Positioning

Not a rental marketplace, travel blog, or corporate relocation service. Boplo owns the individual decision-to-arrival journey.

## Constraints

- Start with three excellent cities, not the world.
- Use dated, transparent estimates and reputable sources.
- Clearly distinguish personal/local insights from verified policy information.
- Position as informational support, not legal or immigration advice.
