# Boplo product direction

Boplo is an AI dating wingman: upload or paste a chat, choose a tone, and get a better reply that still sounds like the user.

## Product sequence

1. Complete the landing page. ✅
2. Proper Boplo accounts: Google and passwordless email, secure sessions, profiles, and a Boplo workspace. **In progress.**
3. Chat-to-reply MVP: screenshot/text upload, conversation extraction, tone and intent controls, three replies, a short chat read, and copy/send.
4. Saved reads and usage credits.
5. Stripe plans and enforced limits.
6. Later: profile reviews, ask-them-out mode, referral loops, notifications, and community only if user demand supports it.

## Product principles

- The core outcome is one confident next message, not generic pickup lines.
- Three free reads should prove value before any payment.
- Boplo is a useful nudge, not a replacement for the user’s personality or judgment.
- Visual identity: midnight black, ice blue, electric blue, clean high-contrast typography.
